01 — Order

What TED & TEDx are

Before the pattern breaks, this is the pattern. Two organizations, one mission: ideas worth spreading.

TED is a nonprofit devoted to spreading ideas, usually in the form of short, powerful talks of eighteen minutes or less. TED began in 1984 as a conference on Technology, Entertainment and Design, and today covers almost all topics — from science to business to global issues — in more than 100 languages.

Meanwhile, independently run TEDx events help share ideas in communities around the world, giving people the opportunity to spark deep conversation at the local level.

TEDxCUSAT is the independently organized TEDx event of Cochin University of Science and Technology, held under license from TED. It gathers CUSAT's brightest minds — students, researchers, alumni, and outside voices — to share ideas that have the power to spark change on campus and beyond.

In full alignment with TED's mission and format, this local event is TEDx, where x = independently organized TED event.

02 — The Glitch

Theme 2026

EVERY REVOLUTION BEGINS AS AN ANOMALY.

TEDxCUSAT is a dynamic platform where the brightest minds of Cochin University of Science And Technology come together to share ideas that have the power to inspire meaningful change.

The universe tends toward disorder. Our systems break, our patterns fail. But in the cracks of failure, the extraordinary emerges. The anomaly is not an error—it is the evolution.

We gather to decode the outliers. The thinkers, builders, and disruptors who refuse the baseline. Join us for talks that rewrite the algorithm of what's possible.

This year, we're not asking speakers to explain how they fit in. We're asking them to explain how they didn't — and what happened next.
